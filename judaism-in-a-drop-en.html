Judaism in a Drop

The temple board of the Vienna City Temple cordially invites you to a glass of wine and learning together.

The “Judaism in a Drop” project is also intended to be a time to get to know other interested community members in a new and better way.

Next dates:

Saturday, October 17, 2026 at 7:00 p.m.: “Humanity & Democracy - New Beginnings & Cohesion”

Saturday, December 12, 2026: "Humanity & Democracy - from darkness to light, Hanukkah sameach!"

Process:

  • (In winter) Shared Havdalah with Chief Cantor Shmuel Barzilai and welcome by Chief Rabbi Jaron Engelmayer
  • Workshop with deep insights into the Jewish worldview and festivals of the annual cycle, but also current topics on democracy and humanity from a Jewish perspective by guest speakers in conversation with Judith Rabfogel-Scheer, the Judaism in a Drop team and participants
  • Drop: moderated wine & talk on current topics, mostly in the context of democracy and humanity from a Jewish perspective, with guest speakers
  • Networking with a drop of wine or grape juice and light snacks
  • Children's program takes place in parallel if at least 5 children register by Wednesday before the event

We look forward to your coming!

Mag.a Judith Rabfogel-Scheer, Dr. Noah Scheer, Lisa Prutscher
