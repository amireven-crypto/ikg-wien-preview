Accessibility statement

This website is a concept demo by PIXELPOP for a redesign of ikg-wien.at – not an official IKG Wien site. It is meant to be usable by everyone – including people who use a screen reader, the keyboard only, magnification or other assistive technology. This statement applies only to this demo at amireven-crypto.github.io/ikg-wien-preview, not to www.ikg-wien.at.

Please send any requests to IKG Wien only through its official website.

Standard and legal basis

Our target is the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2, level AA. This includes the WCAG 2.1 AA requirements of the European standard EN 301 549, which is the reference for the European Accessibility Act (Directive (EU) 2019/882) and the Austrian Accessibility Act (Barrierefreiheitsgesetz, BaFG).

For visitors in Israel, the site also follows Israeli Standard IS 5568 (based on WCAG, level AA) under the Equal Rights for Persons with Disabilities (Service Accessibility Adjustments) Regulations, 2013.

Conformance status

This website is partially conformant with WCAG 2.2 level AA: most content meets the requirements; the exceptions are listed under “Known limitations”.

Assessment method: self-assessment on 18 November 2026 with automated checks (axe-core, Lighthouse) and manual tests – keyboard only, screen widths from 360 px, 200 % zoom, Chrome and Safari (WebKit). The site has not yet been audited by an independent accessibility expert.

What we have done

  • A “Skip to content” link, a clear heading structure and page regions (header, main content, footer).
  • All functions work with the keyboard, with a clearly visible focus indicator.
  • Text and controls meet the WCAG contrast ratios (4.5:1 for text, 3:1 for large text and interface elements).
  • Images have text alternatives; purely decorative images are hidden from screen readers.
  • The layout works on phones from 360 px wide and with 200 % zoom without sideways scrolling; touch targets are at least 24 × 24 px, most are 44 × 44 px.
  • Animations are reduced when your device is set to “reduce motion”, and they can be stopped in the accessibility menu.
  • Each language version sets the correct page language and reading direction (right to left for Hebrew).

Accessibility menu

The button with the accessibility symbol in the page header opens a menu on every page. It works with the mouse, touch and keyboard, and the Esc key closes it. You can enlarge the text in three steps up to 150 %, and switch on high contrast, grayscale, highlighted links, a readable font, wider text spacing, stopped animations, a big cursor and a highlighted focus. The settings are saved only in your browser (local storage), are not sent to us and can be reset at any time.

The menu is an extra. The site itself is built to be accessible without it, and the settings of your browser and operating system (zoom, reduce motion, high contrast) keep working.

Known limitations

  • The content comes from the official website. Some images contain text, and linked PDF documents have not been made accessible.
  • The English, Hebrew and Russian pages are demo translations. Some legal texts are available in German only.
  • Donations, registrations and forms lead to the official IKG Wien pages; their accessibility is not covered by this statement.
  • The background video on the home page is decorative and silent; it has a pause button and stops after two loops.
  • Linked third-party websites and services are not covered by this statement.

Feedback and contact

Did you find a barrier, or do you need content in another format? Please write to us – we will reply as soon as possible.

  • pixelpopsite@gmail.com

Feedback about this concept demo goes to PIXELPOP, who designed it.

Accessibility contact: the PIXELPOP team, by e-mail at the address above.

Enforcement procedure

If you are not satisfied with our answer, you can report the barrier to the Austrian market surveillance authority for the Accessibility Act: Sozialministeriumservice, Landesstelle Oberösterreich, Gruberstraße 63, 4021 Linz.

  • Sozialministeriumservice: report a barrier under the Accessibility Act
  • marktueberwachung-bafg@sozialministeriumservice.gv.at

In Israel, complaints can be made to the Commission for Equal Rights of Persons with Disabilities at the Ministry of Justice.
