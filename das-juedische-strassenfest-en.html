The Jewish street festival

The Jewish street festival is the summer highlight of IKG.Kultur. We celebrate the diversity of Jewish life.

The next Jewish street festival will take place in June 2027.

Jewish street festival

The Jewish street festival takes place annually on a Sunday in June. Admission is free, but photo ID is required for entry.

If you want to learn more about Jewish life, you shouldn't miss the Jewish street festival. The festival has been taking place in June at Vienna's Judenplatz for many years. Over 6,000 visitors now come to this event every year. In addition to numerous information and sales stands about Jewish life, in addition to our stage program with music and dance, there are also offers for children, such as a bouncy castle and face painting. Of course, kosher cuisine is also provided.

The stands

Dozens of Jewish associations and institutions provide an insight into their work at the festival stands. These include youth and charity clubs, stands from the individual Israelitische Kultusgemeinde Wien (Jewish Community of Vienna) departments and many others. In addition, numerous Jewish artists and designers exhibit their art objects, including handicrafts, jewelry, Judaica, pop art and much more - if you like, these can of course be purchased.

Culinary

Your physical well-being is taken care of. From savory to sweet, discover kosher cuisine!

program

Stage shows, live music, children's programs and Jewish folk dancing
