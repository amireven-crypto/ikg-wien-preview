IKG Elections 2022

27.11.2022

IKG/Schmidl

On November 17th, 22nd and 27th, all members of the Israelitische Kultusgemeinde Wien (Jewish Community of Vienna) who were eligible to vote were called to vote for the cultural board. After the votes have been fully counted, the following provisional result is obtained:

Eligible members: 5,490
Votes cast: 3320
Valid votes: 3308
Voter turnout: 60.47%

The votes are distributed among the parties as follows:

ATID - TEAM OSKAR GERMAN
1026 that is 31.02%. This gives the party 8 mandates.
In 2017 the party received 31.57% or 8 mandates.

VBJ SEFARDIM
870 that is 26.30%. This gives the party 7 mandates.
In 2017 the party received 27.06% or 6 mandates.

KEHILLE - TEAM YAACOV FRENKEL
558 that is 16.87%. This gives the party 4 mandates.
In 2017 the party received 11.68% or 3 mandates.

VGJ - ASSOCIATION OF GEORGIAN JEWS
232 that is 7.01%. This gives the party two mandates.
In 2017 the party received 8.04% or 2 mandates.

CHAJ - JEWISH LIFE
222 that is 6.71%. This gives the party 1 mandate.
In 2017 the party received 8.04% or 2 mandates.

ASSOCIATION OF SOCIAL DEMOCRATIC JEWS - AVODA
206 that is 6.23%. This gives the party 1 mandate.
In 2017 the party received 6.14% or 1 mandate.

KHAL ISRAEL
194 that is 5.86%. This gives the party 1 mandate.
In 2017 the party received 7.47% or 2 mandates.

WHO is eligible to vote?

According to Section 164 of the statutes, all community members who have been registered members of the cultural community for at least six months, have turned 18 at the end of the first alternative election day and have had their main residence in the district of the religious community (i.e. in Vienna, Lower Austria, Burgenland, Styria or Carinthia) for at least two years on the day the election is announced have the right to vote.

The deadlines of Section 164 do not apply to persons who were members of a religious community existing in the district in accordance with Section 1 on March 13, 1938.

Parties campaigning for elections in 2022

In order of the number of votes in the last election for the cultural board, these are:

  • ATID - TEAM OSKAR GERMAN
  • VBJ SEFARDIM
  • KEHILLE - TEAM YAACOV FRENKEL
  • CHAJ - JEWISH LIFE
  • VGJ - ASSOCIATION OF GEORGIAN JEWS
  • KHAL ISRAEL
  • ASSOCIATION OF SOCIAL DEMOCRATIC JEWS - AVODA

Couplings

In accordance with Section 183 of the Statute of the Jewish Community, the following electoral lists conclude a coupling agreement:

ATID team Oskar Deutsch, VBJ - Sefardim, Kehille - Team Yaacov Frenkel, VGJ - Association of Georgian Jews, Khal Israel, Association of Social Democratic Jews – AVODA

Each electoral list can join with other lists, which will be announced before the election. When allocating mandates, this means that the coupled lists are viewed as a unit after the number of votes has been determined when distributing mandates, thereby keeping the number of remaining votes not taken into account as low as possible. In a further step, a separate election number is determined within the linkage, which is used to distribute the mandates assigned to the linkage among the lists.

Panel discussion on the occasion of the IKG elections 2022

On November 15th, at 6:30 p.m., the panel discussion on the occasion of the IKG elections 2022, organized by WINA together with the Jewish Austrian Students' Union (JöH), took place. We would like to thank everyone for their active participation and lively exchange.
