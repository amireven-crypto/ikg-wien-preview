Summer brochure 2026

10.06.2026

Summer brochure 2026

Dear children, young people and parents,

Another school year is over and the well-deserved summer holidays are just around the corner!

Together with the Jewish youth and sports clubs, numerous IKG Wien institutions and external partners, JUKO has once again put together a varied summer brochure for you this year.

Look forward to a diverse program with exciting excursions into nature, sporting activities, music courses and many other offers. Whether adventure, exercise, creativity or shared experiences – there is something for everyone!

We hope you enjoy browsing through the brochure, have a wonderful summer and a relaxing holiday.

Your JUKO team

Overview

  1. ZPC summer camp
  2. Chabad Day Camp
  3. Yehuda Halevi Summer Camp
  4. Camp David
  5. Bukharian Community Synagogue
  6. Hashomer Hatzair Sommermachane
  7. Bnei Akiva summer machane
  8. Summer in the Jewish Museum Vienna/Making things – things for resting
  9. Summer in the Jewish Museum Vienna / How we like it!

1. ZPC Summer Camp
Age: 6 - 10 years
Period: 07/06/2026 - 31/07/2026
Time: Monday - Thursday 8:00 a.m. - 5:00 p.m., Friday 8:00 a.m. - 3:20 p.m
Location: ZPC School / Simon-Wiesenthal-Gasse 3, 1020 Wien
Cost: €110 per week

Description:
Guided workshops (morning and afternoon) take place in groups on Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday and Friday. In the morning, the children are informed about the more detailed program for the day. To do this, the children can register for one of the workshops (the procedure is the same as in everyday after-school care).
Prerequisite: Student at ZPC elementary school

More information and registration:
Tel.: 01 2164046240
Email: n.schiffrer@zpc.at

2. Chabad Day Camp
Age: 6 - 10 years
Period: 07/06/2026 - 24/07/2026
Time: Monday - Thursday 8:00 a.m. - 3:45 p.m., Friday 8:00 a.m. - 12:30 p.m
Location: Lauder Chabad School / Rabbi Schneerson Place 1, 1020 Wien
Cost: €380 per child for all weeks

Description:
 A daily summer experience for children of elementary school age (1st - 4th grade) - the camp is full of exciting activities, excursions and supervised by a committed and responsible team, in keeping with the proven Chabad Day Camp tradition.

More information and registration:
Tel.: +43 665 67174830
Email: cdcwien@gmail.com

3. Yehuda Halevi Summer Camp
Age: 6 - 10 years
Period: 08/10/2026 - 28/08/2026
Time: Monday - Thursday 8:30 a.m. - 4:00 p.m., Friday 8:30 a.m. - 2:00 p.m
Location: Sephardic Synagogue / Tempelgasse 7, 1020 Wien
Cost: €180 per week

Description:
 The Jehuda Halevi Music School is the only Jewish music school in Austria and has taken part in numerous competitions with over 100 students and has won first place several times. The Jehuda Halevi Music School Day Camp includes a program led by professional educators, with kosher food and of course a focus on musical activities.

More information and registration:
Tel.: 069910434244
Email: Buero@jh-m.at

4. Camp David
Age: 3 - 6 years
Period: 08/10/2026 - 21/08/2026
Time: Monday - Thursday 9:30 a.m. - 3:30 p.m., Friday 9:30 a.m. - 2:00 p.m
Location: Bet Halevi / Rabbi Schneerson Square 2, 1200 Wien
Cost: €160 for all weeks

Description:
 In the camp, the children are not only looked after but also entertained with cool and religious programs, excursions and much more. There is also a kosher catering with three meals.

More information and registration:
Tel.: 06765111503
Email: david.ustoniatzov@icloud.com

Age: 8 - 18 years
Period: 05.07.2026 - 14.07.2026
Location: Austria
Cost: on request

Description:
 Lots of exciting Peulot, cool outdoor activities and much more await you at this year's summer event! Our Madrichim*ot are looking forward to an unforgettable Machane together with you!

More information and registration:
Tel.: +436765645217
Email: Vienna@hashomerhatzair-eu.com

7. Bnei Akiva Summer Machane
Age: 6 - 18 years
Period: 05.07.2026 - 12.07.2026
Location: Salzburg, Austria
Cost: €500 for the whole week

Description:
 Days full of fun, friends, learning and experiences

More information and registration:
Tel.: +4368120588687
Email: Shaliach@bneiakiva.at

8th Summer in the Jewish Museum Vienna / Making things – things for resting
Age: 6 - 10 years
Period: July 6th, 2026 and July 7th, 2026 from 9:00 a.m. to 1:00 p.m
Age: 10 - 12 years
Period: July 8th, 2026 and July 9th, 2026 from 9:00 a.m. to 1:00 p.m
Location: Jewish Museum Vienna
Cost: on the website

Description:
 Even in the very first Jewish museum in the world, people knew that resting and resting was really important. There was a separate room there called the “Gute Stube” and the theme was the Jewish day of rest Shabbat. Young designers are furnishing this room from scratch with great materials and even greater ideas. The museum and imagination provide inspiration. Ready, rest, go!

What to bring: Comfortable clothing that can get dirty and snack

More information and registration:
 https://www.kinderunikunst.at/de/kinderunikunst-module/kinderunikunst-kreativwoche

9th Summer at the Jewish Museum Vienna / How we like it!
Age: 6 - 14 years
Period: July 13th, 14th, 15th, 16th, 17th, 28th, 29th, 30th and August 2nd, 2026 from 9:30 a.m. to 12:00 p.m
Location: Jewish Museum Vienna
Costs: per child/pupil: €3.00 adults: €15.00 / with holiday play pass: €6.00

Description:
 In the Jewish Museum Vienna, everything has its place on the four floors, but – perhaps there is another way? Smaller, bigger, louder, more comfortable, funnier, more exciting? On a tour of the entire house you will experience the museum as it is. Then you design the museum according to your ideas using boxes, fabrics, foil, modeling clay, chalk, felt and paper.
In cooperation with wienXtra.

More information and registration:
Tel.: +43 1 535 04 31-1538 (Mon - Fri 10:00 a.m. - 3:30 p.m.)
Email: tours@jmw.at

Have a nice summer and stay healthy!
