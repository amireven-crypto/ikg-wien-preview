News from the bar and Bat Mitzvah club 2024/2025

23.06.2025

Credits Raphael Kanfer

8.Unit
Funday & Closing Party – A successful end to a special year

This year's BBMC season came to an end with an eventful Funday in Vienna's Prater - full of action, community and unforgettable moments. The BBMC Boys & Girls enjoyed a day full of fun, energy and togetherness.

Afterwards, things got culinary: the young people showed off their cooking skills at the pizza baking course in the “Rustico” – and of course were also able to taste their homemade creations directly.

Together with the BBMC Boys & Girls and their families, we celebrated the completion of a special journey towards the bar and Bat Mitzvah. This year, the young people dealt intensively with their Jewish identity, explored their family roots, experienced the importance of cohesion and social commitment and gained an insight into the diversity of Jewish traditions, movements and synagogues.

We wish all BBMC boys and girls a warm Mazl Tov!

7.Unit
Synagogue Tour - The diversity of our community

In the last session, the BBMC boys and girls had the opportunity to discover the diverse culture of our community. Together we visited various synagogues and explored the different rites, customs, traditions and history of our community.

Our journey began in the Vienna City Temple, where we explored the melodies and customs of the Ashkenazi rite together with Chief Rabbi Jaron Engelmayer.

We then went to the Jewish Museum at Judenplatz, where Awi Blumenfeld explained to us the history of the first Jewish community in Vienna and we were able to view the excavations of the medieval synagogue.

At our next stop we visited the Sephardic synagogue in Tempelgasse, where Benjamin Gilkarov explained the Sephardic rite to the BBMC boys and girls and led them through the prayer rooms.

A special highlight was the visit to the Orthodox synagogue in Große Mohrengasse. There the children learned more about kashrut and had the opportunity to dip their new, cool BBMC cups in the mikveh.

Our tour concluded at the Bahur Tov restaurant, where we were treated to delicious specialties such as plov. The children were enthusiastic and learned a lot about the different rites, customs and stories of Judaism.

This experience highlighted the diversity of the community and strengthened our solidarity!

6.Unit
Jewish empowerment

In the 6th BBMC unit we immersed ourselves in the fascinating world of Jewish heroines and heroes. This time we also had Madrichot from BBYO with us as support. Together we explored what “Jewish Empowerment” means and what positive influence you can have both inside and outside the Jewish community and how you can do good as a community and with initiatives.

The children got to know important Jewish personalities who have shaped history with their commitment in the areas of religion, science, politics, culture and art and examined their biographies. At the end, they were able to let their creativity run wild and design their own superheroes - with special powers that make the world a better place.

IKG. Youth

5.Unit
Challe Bake and Shared Shabbat

The 5th BBMC unit was all about Shabbat. On Thursday, the participants got in the mood by baking challot together and making Shabbat candles. Together with Michal Grünberger, they not only learned about the special features and routine of Shabbat, but also traditional Shabbat songs, which created a special atmosphere the following day when they ate together on Friday. On Friday, the young people experienced a common Tfila and a celebratory Kabbalat Shabbat in the city temple. The dinner together in the company of Chief Rabbi Jaron Engelmayer was a successful conclusion to this double unit. An unforgettable experience full of learning, solidarity and beautiful traditions!

4.Unit
Jewish roots
In the 4th unit we devoted ourselves to the exciting topic of Jewish roots and family history. This time we received valuable support from Madrichim*ot of Jad be Jad. Together, the BBMC kids immersed themselves in the diversity of our community and discovered the different family traditions of the participants. As part of the unit, they created their own family tree and a personalized family box. In these boxes, the children collected special memories: photos, family recipes and stories about their families' cultural heritage. The result is a unique treasure that makes the meaning of origin and tradition tangible.

3.Unit
Charity and Zdaka

In the 3rd BBMC unit we dealt with the topics of charity and Zdaka. This time we also had Madrichim*ot from Bnei Akiva with us as support. Together we talked about charity and mitzvot and took a closer look at Jewish aid organizations.

We immediately put what we had learned into practice and visited the residents of the Maimonides Center. Because the most beautiful and valuable thing you can give is time. Of course we didn't come empty-handed.

The BBMC kids decorated muffins, created pictures with personal messages and made a flower bed. A highlight was the singing performance by the BBMC group, for which they rehearsed "Am Israel Chai" and performed it with a dance choreo. Finally, the children designed their own Zdaka box to take home.

A day full of good deeds and beautiful moments!

Many thanks to Bnei Akiva and the Maimonides Center for the support!

2.Unit
Who am I - Jewish Identity
 In the second unit, the BBMC boys and girls dealt with their Jewish identity and learned about the different facets and expressions of Judaism. Using items they had brought with them, the young people explained their personal connection to Judaism - from Star of David necklaces, Siddurim, Channukiot to items from Israel, everything was there and represented the broad diversity of Judaism in Vienna and the young generation.
Afterwards, the BBMC boys and girls were able to express themselves creatively and represented their Jewish identity in a craft activity and gave it personal expression. Questions were answered such as: "When is your Jewish birthday? What does Judaism mean to you? What is your favorite holiday" and much more. The young people were able to get to know each other better and learned a lot of new things about their friends.

1st unit, October 13th
Me, you, us - the big get to know each other
 Our first introductory meeting with the BBMC class of 2024/25 was a complete success! With over 40 kids, this is the largest BBMC group since the program was founded in Vienna. We had a day full of fun and exciting activities. We started with fun getting-to-know-you games that immediately put you in a good mood. Particularly interesting was guessing old childhood photos, which revealed many funny stories and helped us learn more about the personalities of our BBMC boys and girls.
Another highlight was the creation of a “BBMC Family Tree”. Equipped with canvases, craft supplies and Polaroid cameras, the young people were able to give free rein to their creativity. It was nice to see how they implemented their ideas and created great works of art. Of course there was pizza as a refreshment. Overall, it was a wonderful start to an exciting BBMC year and we look forward to seeing how our 2024/25 cohort develops!
