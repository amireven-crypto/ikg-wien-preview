The Citizens' Parliament for the restoration and renovation of the city temple

11.09.2025

The second citizens' parliament on the upcoming restoration and renovation of the city temple took place on September 10th. The KENH architectural firm presented the plans, the implementation of which will begin in October this year.

The city temple has always been a place where very different people with diverse needs come together. Doing justice to these was a central guiding principle in the planning phase. Numerous discussions with community members and other stakeholders made it possible to record wishes and suggestions and integrate them into the planning.

In addition to the comprehensive restoration and renovation measures, acoustics, lighting, heating and cooling systems as well as accessibility are being optimized. The color concept of the temple was also carefully examined: the dome in particular has shown different colors and patterns over the past 200 years. Ultimately, the decision was made to keep the current, familiar shade of blue.

In addition, concrete improvements will be implemented: an area for halachic hand washing will be created, and the cloakroom will be adapted to the needs of the community and guests. The benches will be ergonomically renovated and equipped with reading lamps so that there is enough brightness to read the prayer books even in dimmed light.

Following the presentation, there was an opportunity to ask questions and contribute ideas. Numerous participants took advantage of this opportunity, resulting in a lively and constructive exchange in which many concerns could be discussed.
