Sold out house and big laughs: Zach Margs inspires in the Urania

19. 03. 26.
Share

Embedded media of the original page is not loaded in this demo (data protection - no request to third parties).

Open externally: embedded media (opens in new tab)


On March 18th, Zach Margs and his “ZACH'S BACK: EUROPE TOUR” ensured a completely sold out evening at the Urania in Vienna. Tickets had not been available for weeks - and the audience was rewarded with an energetic, pointed comedy program.

With a sharp wit and a lot of self-irony, Margs took his audience on a humorous journey through everyday situations and Jewish-Israeli lives. Tales of failed Hebrew translations affecting his dating life in unexpected ways, as well as insights into his father's role in his social media career, brought plenty of laughs.

A central moment of the evening was the interactive audience survey - this is where the mood reached its peak.

Well-known characters from his social media videos - including the taxi driver and ELAL security guards - also found their place in the program and, with their high recognition value, caused laughter in the room throughout.

The British-Jewish comedian impressed with speed, charm and spot-on humor that doesn't separate cultural differences, but connects them. After international success and millions of online views, Zach Margs celebrated a successful Vienna premiere this evening - and left an audience that saw him off with enthusiastic applause.

A cooperation between IKG.Kultur, IKG Youth and Sport, Israel Explained.
