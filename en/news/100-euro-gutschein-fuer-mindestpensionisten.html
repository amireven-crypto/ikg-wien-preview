100 euro voucher for minimum pensioners

05. 12. 22.
Share

After the successful school launch campaign, in which more than 300 school children from families with low incomes received shopping vouchers, the big Hanukkah aid campaign for the older generation in our community now follows: minimum pensioners aged 60 and over (born in 1962) receive a 100 euro voucher from REWE (Billa, BIPA, etc.).

The relief campaign is financed by donations to TMICHA, the IKG Wien relief association. We ask everyone who can afford it to support the support of elderly people at risk of poverty in our community. As a small thank you, every donor receives the beautiful “Hanukkah package”. It includes a Hanukkah guide with blessings and songs, as well as a magnetic travel Hanukkah with colorful candles (from a donation of at least 26 euros).

Donate here

 

Who is eligible?

Community members aged 60 and over (born in 1962) who meet one of the following additional criteria are eligible:

- a pension with a compensatory allowance
- receive a minimum income.

Please note that when applying for the 100 euro voucher from the Hanukkah relief campaign, you must submit the relevant notifications from the PVA or MA40.

How do I apply for the 100 euro voucher from the Hanukkah relief campaign?

All community members will receive a letter in the coming days. Applications can be submitted up to and including December 19th. 2022 (the postmark applies).

Please fill out the form enclosed with the letter and enclose a copy of your current notice from the pension insurance or minimum security (MA40).

Please send the letter back with sufficient postage to the following address:

Israelitische Kultusgemeinde Wien z.H. TMICHA
Seitenstettengasse 4, 1010 Wien

If you have any questions or need assistance, please feel free to contact Shiran Nanikashvili at 0664 887 639 57. You can also contact us via email at office@tmicha.at.
