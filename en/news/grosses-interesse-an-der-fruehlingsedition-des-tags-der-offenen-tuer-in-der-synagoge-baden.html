Great interest in the spring edition of the open day at the Baden Synagogue

01. 06. 26.
Share

Embedded media of the original page is not loaded in this demo (data protection - no request to third parties).

Open externally: embedded media (opens in new tab)

This year's open day on May 31st in the Baden Synagogue once again showed how lively and open encounters can be - and at the same time set new accents. For the first time, the event took place in the warm season, which gave the entire afternoon a particularly inviting atmosphere. The inner courtyard of the synagogue became a place for exchange, lingering and meeting.

Shortly after admission, the area was filled with interested visitors of all ages. Many took the opportunity not only to get to know the synagogue and the center for intercultural encounters, but also to talk to each other. The atmosphere of the day was particularly characterized by open cooperation: questions were asked, experiences were shared and new perspectives were gained.

The dialogue lectures were met with great interest. Particularly during “Ask the Rabbi,” a lively exchange developed in which state rabbi Schlomo Hofmeister answered the audience’s questions openly and approachably. The other program items also offered space for encounters and new approaches - be it the humorous introduction to the Yiddish language with Roman Grinberg or the lecture on women in Judaism with Eidel Malowicki.

A special innovation was the program in the courtyard of the synagogue, which was very positively received by the guests. Between kosher appetizers and selected wines, there was plenty of time for personal conversations in a relaxed atmosphere. It was precisely this interplay between the content of the program and the open gathering that gave the event a new character and made the day noticeably livelier.

The atmospheric finale was the concert by the Yentes Kuzines together with Roman Grinberg in the ZiB. Through the sonorous interplay of their voices, Sarah Leicht, Hannah Tamar, Feli Stein and Patricia Herzberger gave traditional Yiddish songs a modern musical interpretation and received a standing ovation from the audience.

An afternoon that was under the patronage of Mayor Carmen Jeitler-Cincelli, who also attended the event in person, ended with music, humor and tangible joy in shared experiences. Many guests will remember the open day for a long time - not least because it showed how enriching real exchange can be.
