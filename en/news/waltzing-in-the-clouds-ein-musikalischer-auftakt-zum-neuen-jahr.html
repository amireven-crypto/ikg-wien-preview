“Waltzing in the Clouds”: A musical start to the new year

25. 09. 26.
Share

Embedded media of the original page is not loaded in this demo (data protection - no request to third parties).

Open externally: embedded media (opens in new tab)

Full house and great atmosphere in the ZiB: On September 24th, a special musical evening took place in Baden on the occasion of the Rosh Hashanah festival, the Jewish New Year. The ZiB was filled to capacity and the audience enjoyed an evening full of music, humor and Viennese lifestyle.

Under the title “Waltzing in the Clouds”, singer Ethel Merhaut, together with Aliosha Biz, Belush Korenyi and Clemens Gigacher, took the audience on a musical journey through Vienna in the 1920s. Songs and melodies were heard that were decisively influenced by Jewish musicians and are still associated with Viennese musical culture today.

Works by Hermann Leopoldi, Fritz Kreisler, Georg Kreisler and Hugo Wiener were reinterpreted with charm, musical virtuosity and a good dose of Viennese humor. Between Viennese songs, chansons, cabaret and operetta, a varied program was created that made you smile, remember and think.

The audience thanked them with lots of applause and made the evening a completely successful concert evening. “Waltzing in the Clouds” was an atmospheric musical start to the new year and a wonderful encounter with the rich Jewish-Viennese cultural heritage.
