Jewish street festival at Judenplatz thrilled with music, dancing and encounters

15. 06. 26.
Share

Open externally: embedded media (opens in new tab)

On June 14th, in pleasant summer weather - it was not too hot and not too cold - Judenplatz once again became the scene of a lively festival of encounters. From the early afternoon, numerous visitors took the opportunity to celebrate the diversity of Jewish life in Vienna together. The sunny atmosphere, the varied program and the lively comings and goings on the square made Judenplatz a place full of joy of life until the evening hours.

orf contribution to the street festival

The performances by the Brothers Meirov Band with their traditional Bukharian drumming and the Noya Showband with lively Israeli dance music provided musical highlights. With their rousing performances, they created an exuberant atmosphere that invited many guests to linger. The dance group Tirkedu Rikudei-Am also thrilled the audience with their performance and contributed to the festive atmosphere. There was always dancing, clapping and celebrating in front of the stage - a sign of how much the music brought people together.

There was great interest in the numerous information, art and sales stands. Jewish organizations, initiatives and creative professionals presented their work and offered insights into the diversity of Jewish life and cultural work in Vienna. Many visitors took the opportunity to exchange ideas, discover new things and talk to the exhibitors.

The street festival also had a lot to offer in terms of cuisine. Guests were able to enjoy a wide selection of kosher foods and sweet delicacies. The gastronomic offerings were welcomed throughout the day. In addition to shawarma, falafel, sabich and traditional Bukharian specialties, sweet delicacies also ensured enjoyment.

There was also a lot to experience for the youngest visitors. The bouncy castle, imaginative make-up stations and the popular bubble station ensured smiling faces and lots of entertainment. This made the street festival an experience for the whole family.

The Jewish Street Festival 2026 once again showed how important places for meeting, cultural exchange and celebrating together are. For one day, Judenplatz was transformed into an open and lively meeting place where community, diversity and joy of life were the focus.
