“Oz's List”: A special film evening on October 7th

08. 10. 26.
Share

© IKG.Culture

A sold-out metro cinema, a film about extraordinary courage and the personal encounter with a person who experienced the events of October 7th, 2023: On October 7th, IKG.Kultur, together with the Jewish Film Club and the Embassy of the State of Israel, presented the documentary “Oz's List” - three years after the terrorist attack on October 7th, 2023.

The focus of the film is Oz Davidian. On the morning of October 7th, he repeatedly drove to the area around the Nova Festival and saved more than 120 young people from the terrorists. The fact that Oz Davidian was a guest at the Metrokino together with director Gidi Maron gave the evening a very special and touching dimension.

In the film discussion that followed, moderated by Frank Stern, Oz Davidian and Gidi Maron spoke about the events of that day, the making of the film and the experiences that still have an impact today.

The experience left deep traces. Oz Davidian speaks openly about how the post-traumatic effects of October 7th will stay with him for the rest of his life. And yet he also draws something positive from his experience: the more than 120 people he saved and whom he affectionately calls “his children” moved on three years later - some got married, others had children of their own. Oz has become part of her extended family. For him, this is exactly a sign that new life and hope can emerge from a day of unimaginable horror.

At the end of the evening, the audience thanked Oz Davidian with sustained applause and a standing ovation - for his extraordinary courage and tireless efforts with which he saved so many lives that day.

Anyone who couldn't be there for this special evening has the opportunity to watch “Oz's List” on ORF ON. The film was also broadcast on ORF 2 as part of the “Weltjournal”.
