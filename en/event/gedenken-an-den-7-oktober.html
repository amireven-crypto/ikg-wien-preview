Commemoration of October 7th

Date & Time: 05.10.2025, 18:00

Location: Judenplatz
Share

Event Details

Date & Time: 05.10.2025, 18:00

Location: Judenplatz

Add it to your calendar

Add to Google Cal Add to iCal

The anniversary of October 7, 2023 is approaching, which represents a drastic turning point for all Jewish communities worldwide and the entire civilized world. The biggest pogrom since the Shoah is painfully burned into our collective memory. Since October 7th falls on the Sukkot holiday this year, we will come together on Sunday, October 5th, 2025, at 6 p.m. to remember those murdered and to remember the hostages who are still being kidnapped.

Around the joint commemoration, the Israelite Community, together with many organizations in Vienna, is taking action to raise the visibility of the hostages.

Before the start of Simchat Torah, on October 13th at 6 p.m., there will be a joint memorial for community members in the City Temple.

Invite your friends and acquaintances to the rally on October 5th!
