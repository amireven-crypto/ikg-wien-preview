IKG.Kultur - The Cultural Department of the Jewish Community of Vienna

Vibrant, diverse, and connecting – this is the guiding principle of the cultural program of IKG.Kultur, the cultural department of the Jewish Community of Vienna.

Our cultural work is exceptionally multifaceted, bridging the rich Jewish cultures of Central- and Eastern-Europe before the Shoah, with the present and a future international Jewish culture.

Through events such as the Jewish Street Festival, the Cantors’ Concert and the Jewish New Year’s Concert we showcase the vitality and diversity of Jewish culture while fostering dialogue between Jewish and non-Jewish audiences.

Especially in light of the renewed rise of antisemitism worldwide, we are committed to openness and encounter. By promoting contemporary Jewish art and culture we seek to encourage understanding, mutual respect, and the prevention of prejudice.

Experience Viennese Jewish Culture

Jewish Street Festival

With the street festival we celebrate the diversity of Jewish life. Numerous Jewish organizations and institutions offer insights into their work. Alongside stage shows, live music, and a children’s program there will be culinary delights from kosher cuisine and Jewish folk dancing.

Learn more

Jewish New Year’s Concerts

Since 2022 it has become a fixed date in the cultural calendar. Everyone is warmly invited to celebrate life and diversity together with the Jewish community.

Learn more

Cantor's Concert

With the annual Cantors’ Concert Viennese audiences can experience the art of Chazanut—cantorial singing—live. Featuring Chief Cantor Shmuel Barzilai and a special guest.

Learn more

Welcome to the Synagogue of Baden

At the Baden Synagogue and the cultural center "Zentrum für Interkulturelle Begegnung - ZIB" numerous events take place every year.
