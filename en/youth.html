Youth

Share

Vienna offers a wide variety of Jewish youth activities ranging from religious education and leadership development to sports activities and civic engagement. The youth commission of Vienna's Jewish communities offers activities for all young Jews in Vienna. For more information on events and news please visit the German language page.
