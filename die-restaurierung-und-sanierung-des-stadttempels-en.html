Restoration and renovation of the city temple

14.09.2026

Image source: IKG/Morgensztern

On September 9, 2026, two days before the start of the High Holidays, the Jewish community ceremoniously reopened its spiritual center in Seitenstettengasse after ten and a half months of construction.

Report on the celebrations

Below are the pictures of the newly restored and renovated city temple

Image source: IKG/Morgensztern

The City Temple is the spiritual center of the Jewish community in Vienna and the largest synagogue in Austria. It was the only major synagogue in Vienna to survive the November pogrom in 1938. In 2026, the city temple built by Josef Kornhäusl will celebrate its 200th anniversary. The restoration and renovation of the Vienna City Temple makes this spiritual and cultural-historical center of Austrian Jewry preserved and ready for the future.


Visits and prayers

  • Visits to the IKG Wien and deliveries will be made via the Rabensteig 3 during the renovation work.
  • Prayers will now take place regularly again in the city temple.
  • Guided tours will be offered from winter 2026.

Review of the restoration and renovation of the city temple

July 2026

Restoration and renovation work at the Vienna City Temple is still in full swing. Work is being carried out in parallel in several areas of the building. The wall paneling is being installed, the technical infrastructure and electrics are being installed and completed, floors are being re-laid, filling and painting are being carried out.

After the lantern was completed, the dome has now been painted and shines again in the usual shade of blue. The staircases to the galleries have already been painted, the columns have been completely restored and the ceilings in the synagogue have been worked on.

The substructures of the new paneling, behind which the building services are also hidden, have already been installed, and the final wall paneling has already been installed in places on the first floor. Carpets and acoustic panels have already been laid on the second floor and the familiar furniture has been put back in place, but not yet fixed - this is happening in conjunction with the stair lift planned there and a new row of seats.

The sukkah is complete and waiting to be festively decorated next fall.

The air-conducting steel structures for the armchairs on the ground floor have also been installed; the new stone floor is being worked on. With God’s help, nothing stands in the way of a grand opening in September.

The remaining weeks are scheduled to the minute and work and assembly are being carried out diligently on site.

March 2026

The construction site is in full swing and all trades are working in parallel in different areas of the building. The most visible progress at the moment is the dismantling of the large interior scaffolding. This made new work sections accessible and the view of the restored structures in the light dome is now clear.

As part of the ongoing measures, the old locks were removed. At the same time, extensive preparations are being made for the technical infrastructure: slots are being made for the electrical system and chiseling work is being carried out for the building services. In addition, the old door frame was uncovered, as was a historical floor area in the Bima area - a special find, also to the delight of the Federal Monuments Office.

Our special thanks go to the federal government, the city of Vienna and all donors who each finance a third of this project. If you would also like to support this project, we would be happy to receive your donation: https://www.stadttempel.at/

Intensive construction measures are currently being implemented in the entrances and side rooms of the synagogue, which primarily serve to ensure accessibility and safety. The entrances will receive new stairs; Stair lifts will also be installed in relevant places. The toilet facilities will be comprehensively modernized and, where necessary, designed to be barrier-free. Fire curtains, locks and emergency lighting are implemented in accordance with applicable regulations.

Overall, the work continues to proceed according to plan and with visible progress in several key areas at the same time. After Passover (Pesach), the rough work will be largely completed so that the interior work can get started.

December 2025

The restoration and renovation work in the Vienna City Temple of Israelitische Kultusgemeinde Wien (Jewish Community of Vienna) (IKG) began as planned on October 20th and is progressing without delays so far. The work is on schedule; The costs are also currently within the estimated budget.

During the construction work, the historic stone bases and the historic stone flooring were exposed.

The measures taken so far primarily include demolition work and the creation of necessary breakthroughs. Construction work is currently concentrating on the technical installations in the building's lantern. The extensive scaffolding, which takes up almost the entire interior, should be removed as soon as possible.

October 2025

On October 17, 2025, the official start of the restoration work took place in the Vienna City Temple with a press event. An art project by Gottfried Helnwein was also presented, which supports the renovation. Ten children from the Jewish community were portrayed in a casting; One of them will depict Helnwein in a large-format painting that will be shown in 2026 for the 200th anniversary and auctioned off to benefit restoration. After completion, the image will be affixed to wooden panels from the city temple, which were symbolically given to the artist. On this occasion, the KENH architectural firm presented the restoration plans, the aim of which is to preserve the historical heritage and secure the future of the building. The costs are 10.5 million euros; Donations are still possible via www.stadttempel.at.

September 2025 – Alef Alef

From October 20, 2025, the operation of the Alef Alef restaurant will be adjusted during the restoration of the City Temple. Prayers take place in the community center, kiddushim as standing receptions in the restaurant area. Shabbat meals remain possible after registration, as do smaller private or family events.

September 2025 – Meeting of the Cultural Board

On September 15, 2025, architect Natalie Neubauer reported to the cultural board about the specific construction plans for the renovation of the city temple. The work is being carried out in coordination with the Federal Monuments Office and includes technical modernization and accessibility. President Oskar Deutsch announced that the city of Vienna and the federal government would each contribute a third of the 10.5 million euros; The first phase of the project is financed with donations. This phase includes the restoration of the temple, while phase two - renovation of the community center and the facade - will come later. The board approved the implementation.

September 2025 – Citizens’ Parliament

The second citizens' parliament on the restoration and renovation of the city temple took place on September 10, 2025. The KENH architectural firm presented the final plans, the implementation of which will begin in October. Improvements to acoustics, lighting, heating and cooling systems and accessibility are planned. The color concept will be retained with the previous blue tone of the dome. New additions include an area for halachic hand washing, an adapted cloakroom and ergonomic benches with reading lamps. At the event, community members were able to ask questions and make suggestions.

January 2025

On January 28, 2025, Mayor Michael Ludwig visited the Vienna City Temple and announced that the City of Vienna would cover a third of the approximately 10 million euro restoration costs. The federal government covers another third and the remaining third is financed through donations. The renovation includes monument preservation, improving the acoustics, the indoor climate and accessibility as well as modernizing the security system. The city temple, built in 1826 and the only synagogue in Vienna to survive during the November pogroms in 1938, is to be fully restored by its 200th anniversary in 2026.

September 2024

On September 12, 2024 , the restoration plans for the Vienna City Temple were presented at a press conference. In addition to IKG President Oskar Deutsch and Secretary General Benjamin Nägele, architects Natalie Neubauer and Eric-Emanuel Tschaikner from KENH presented the planned measures. Construction is scheduled to begin in fall 2025 and completion in fall 2026. The work includes restoring the historical appearance, improving acoustics, indoor climate, accessibility and security systems as well as renovating the community infrastructure. The city temple, built in 1826 and the oldest active synagogue in Austria, is intended to be preserved for the future. The public is invited to donate; A star can be dedicated to the dome from 2,500 euros.

March 2024

In March 2024, the Israelitische Kultusgemeinde Wien (Jewish Community of Vienna) cultural board unanimously decided to commission the architect Natalie Neubauer and the architect Eric-Emanuel Tschaikner (KENH Architekten ZT GmbH) to plan the restoration and renovation of the Vienna City Temple. The goal is to renovate the synagogue in time for the 200th anniversary in 2026. The work includes the renovation of building services, fire protection measures, accessibility, acoustics, ventilation and heating. Since the building is a listed building, all work is carried out in coordination with the Federal Monuments Office. The community center, which is not listed, is to be adapted to the needs of the community. Financing comes from public funding and fundraising, not from the current IKG budget. Neubauer waives ten percent of her fee.
